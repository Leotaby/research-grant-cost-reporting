# Cost reporting guidelines

*For the staff and the administrative office of an EU-funded research project (Horizon Europe).
Italian version: [linee_guida_rendicontazione.md](linee_guida_rendicontazione.md). Sources in
[rules_reference.md](rules_reference.md). The signed grant agreement and the call conditions prevail.*

## 1. The seven conditions (Grant Agreement Art. 6.1)

A cost is eligible only if it is actually incurred; incurred during the action; declared in a
budget category; connected to the action in Annex 1 and necessary for it; identifiable and
verifiable; compliant with national tax, labour and social security law; and reasonable,
justified and in line with sound financial management.

## 2. Personnel

- Cost = daily rate × days worked on the action, rounded to the nearest half-day.
- Daily rate = annual personnel costs / 215.
- **No more than 215 days a year per person across all EU grants**: check the days already
  declared on other EU projects before reporting.
- If the financial year is not closed yet, use the daily rate of the last closed year.
- **Every month**: a timesheet (or, for people working only on the action, a monthly
  day-declaration) **signed by the person and the supervisor**. Unsigned days cannot be declared.
- Suggested deadline: last month's records signed by the 5th of the following month.

## 3. Travel (C.1)

Follow the institution's travel rules; if they allow economy class, a business-class fare must be
justified or declared at the economy fare. Keep tickets, boarding passes, hotel invoices and the
meeting agenda showing the link to the project.

## 4. Equipment (C.2)

Declare **depreciation only**: the months of use in the period, times the share of use for the
action, from the date the asset is available. Never the full price unless the call provides the
full-cost option. Record each asset in the inventory with delivery date, useful life and share of use.

## 5. Other goods and services (C.3) and subcontracting (B)

- Invoices addressed to the institution, referring to the project (in Italy, the CUP), paid by
  traceable means.
- VAT is eligible only if the institution cannot recover it.
- Subcontracting (third parties carrying out action tasks) must be set out in Annex 1; otherwise it
  needs an amendment or approval under the simplified procedure.

## 6. Purchasing (Italy: D.Lgs. 36/2023)

Supplies and services below EUR 140 000: direct award. From EUR 140 000 up to the EU thresholds:
negotiated procedure with at least five operators. Always: best value for money, no conflict of
interest, a complete procurement file.

## 7. Never eligible (Art. 6.3)

Interest owed, debt service, provisions for future losses, exchange losses, bank charges on
transfers from the granting authority, excessive or reckless expenditure, deductible VAT, costs
incurred during a suspension, and costs already declared under another EU grant.

## 8. Indirect costs

A flat rate on eligible direct costs (25% in Horizon Europe, excluding subcontracting), with no
supporting documents needed. Every ineligible direct cost also loses the 25% on top of it.

## 9. Certificate and records

- Horizon Europe: a **certificate on the financial statements (CFS)** comes with the final report
  once the requested EU contribution reaches EUR 430 000. Budget the audit fee.
- Keep every supporting document for **5 years after the balance payment** (3 years for grants up to
  EUR 60 000).

## 10. Check calendar

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly | Signed time records; invoices booked with the project reference |
| Quarterly | Run `python -m grantcheck`: 215-day cap, depreciation, VAT, missing documents |
| Before each periodic report | Fix the findings, request approvals (subcontracts, amendments), prepare the financial statement |
| Final report | CFS if required |
